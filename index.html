Steam Page

Use guns to shoot enemies, monsters, teammates, yourself, and most importantly the giant soccer ball.

Charlio Ball is a soccer-based top-down arena shooter. Use endless combinations of abilities, guns, and passives to create a unique build, kill your enemies, and dribble the ball into their goal.


Key features

  • Lightning Fast Action!

    Nearly every ability moves you, your opponent, or the ball, turning every encounter into a chaotic pinball of players and projectiles.

  • Every ability, Every Weapon, Available for Every Player.

    Absolutely everything is available to every player with no class restrictions. If you pick 3 abilities and a gun, you are likely playing something literally never before seen.

  • Team-based Strategy

    Coordinate with your allies in every situation. Who is healing, who is using crowd control, and who is focusing on the ball?

  • Destroy the Towers, Score the Goal

    Take down enemy towers to open up the field and create scoring opportunities.

Behind the game

Charlie Arleth

I'm a solo dev who loves competition, movement, and creativity in games. I live for the excitement of bonding with friends over a match and I hope to create that feeling for other people. I will always prioritize making the game that I want to play and I'm happy to share that game with everyone

How it started

I started work on Charlio Ball 6 years ago. It was the start of the pandemic and I wanted something my friends and I could play that would keep us all in touch, so I made a fast, casual, and zany game. I didn't intend to ever release it but they showed it to their friends who showed it to theirs, and people were interested enough that I never stopped working on it.
